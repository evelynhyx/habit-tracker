Monkey's Guide to Using Habit Tracker

You seem lost! Let Monkey help you.

If you have a goal or habit in mind that you actually want to achieve, this is the perfect website for you!

To get started, click on "New Habit" text to set your habit—whether it's drinking water, exercising, or reading a book. Each day, click on the corresponding number to mark it as complete. You can also click on the day again to mark it as incomplete. **You can't mark anything after the current day!

To the right of the habit title, you can see how many days you've completed your habit out of the total number of days in the current month. The habit tracker automatically updates the month so you don't have to! Finally, to reset your habit tracker, simply click the "Reset" button. Your progress is saved, so it'll still be there even after you close the tab and revisit the site.

With this, you will be able to easily reach your goal in no time. What are you waiting for? Get tracking!
